Why this exists

One of Smrita Jain’s federal clients asked to change its wording from AI to SI. That raised a question: if the words change, what changes in what people find, read and talk about?

AI Mountain Man follows both words around the world, one day and one jump at a time.

How it counts

  • 1 · News counts news articles that contain each term, refreshed every 30 minutes, from the GDELT Project. USA uses US-based outlets only.
  • 2 · Wiki counts human page views of the English Wikipedia articles Artificial intelligence and Superintelligence (Wikimedia API). Counts arrive a day late and have no country split.
  • Live wire watches every new public Bluesky post through the free Jetstream feed, plus new Hacker News items every 15 seconds. It counts only while this page is open and pauses when the tab is hidden.
  • 3 · HN counts Hacker News posts and comments, via HN Search by Algolia. Global only.

Reading it fairly

These are samples of public attention, not every word typed on Earth. The big ticker is a live pace estimate: the last three full days’ average, spread across today and never below today’s measured count. Days are UTC.
